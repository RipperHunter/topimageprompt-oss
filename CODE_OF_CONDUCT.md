# Code of conduct

PromptVault OSS is committed to a respectful, welcoming, and harassment-free community.

Contributors must communicate constructively, respect differing viewpoints and experience levels, give actionable feedback, and focus disagreements on the work. Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, and publication of another person's private information are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when behavior is unsafe or persistently disruptive. Serious conduct concerns should be reported privately through the repository's security contact rather than debated in a public issue.

This policy applies in project repositories, issue trackers, reviews, discussions, and project-related public events.
